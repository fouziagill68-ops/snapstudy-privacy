Privacy Policy for SnapStudy - AI Tutor

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SnapStudy - AI Tutor ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address and basic profile information (name, if provided) when you sign in using Email/Password or Google Sign-In, via Firebase Authentication.

Photos and Content You Provide

  • Photos you take or select from your gallery of homework, textbooks, or study material, which you submit to the App to receive AI-generated tutoring, answers, or feedback.
  • Any follow-up questions you type in relation to that content.

These photos and questions are sent securely to our backend (Google Cloud Functions), which uses Google's Vertex AI (Gemini models) to generate a response. We do not permanently store your photos on our servers; they are used only to generate your tutoring response.

Usage Information

  • Basic usage data such as your daily free-analysis count (stored locally on your device), selected language preference, and app settings.

Advertising Data

  • The App displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and other data to show relevant ads and measure ad performance, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate you and provide access to the App.
  • To process your submitted photos/questions and generate AI tutoring responses.
  • To enforce daily usage limits for free-tier users.
  • To display advertisements that help keep the App free to use.
  • To improve the App's features and reliability.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Functions) — for login and processing your requests.
  • Google Vertex AI — to generate AI tutoring responses from your submitted content.
  • Google AdMob — to serve advertisements within the App.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies and may be located outside your country.

4. Data Storage and Security

Account authentication data is stored securely via Firebase Authentication. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Children's Privacy

SnapStudy - AI Tutor is intended for general audiences, including students. We do not knowingly collect more information from children than is necessary to provide the App's tutoring features. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided personal information without consent may contact us to request deletion.

6. Your Choices

  • You may log out or delete your account at any time.
  • You may contact us to request deletion of your account data.
  • You may control ad personalization through your device's Google Ads settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
